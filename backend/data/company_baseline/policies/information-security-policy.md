# Information Security Policy (سياسة أمن المعلومات)

- Document ID: POL-001
- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2025-11-10
- Owner: IT Security Lead
- Approved by: Executive Management
- Applicable regulation: NCA ECC-1:2018

## 1. Purpose (الغرض)

This policy sets out how Eastern Industrial Company protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information, its industrial control products and its production systems. It is the top-level cybersecurity policy from which all other policies and procedures derive.

## 2. Scope (النطاق)

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and suppliers with access to company information or systems, to all IT and operational technology (OT) assets at the Dammam Plant, and to the ICU-200 Industrial Control Unit and SCADA-Lite Monitoring System product lines.

## 3. Governance and roles

- Executive Management approves this policy and provides the resources needed to apply it.
- The IT Security Lead owns this policy, maintains the cybersecurity strategy and reports on its status to Executive Management every quarter.
- The OT Engineering Lead is responsible for cybersecurity in production systems and product firmware.
- The Compliance Officer tracks applicable regulatory requirements and verifies compliance.
- Every employee is responsible for following the acceptable use rules in section 4.

## 4. Asset management and acceptable use

- All information assets are recorded in the asset register with a named owner and a classification level.
- Assets may be used only for approved business purposes.
- Removable media, personal devices and unapproved cloud services must not be connected to company systems without written approval from the IT Security Lead.

## 5. Risk management

- A cybersecurity risk assessment is carried out at least once a year, and whenever a major change is made to systems, products or the production line.
- Risks are recorded in the risk register with an owner, a treatment decision and a target date.

## 6. Third-party and supplier security

- Cybersecurity requirements are included in every supplier contract and service level agreement.
- Suppliers with access to company systems are assessed before onboarding and reviewed every year.

## 7. Incident response

- The company maintains a cybersecurity incident response plan with defined roles, classification levels and escalation paths.
- Incidents are recorded, classified and reported to the National Cybersecurity Authority as required.
- The plan is tested at least once a year through a tabletop exercise.

## 8. Business continuity

- Backups of critical systems and production controller configurations are taken and restored on a tested schedule.
- Cybersecurity requirements are part of the business continuity plan for the Dammam Plant.

## 9. Review and compliance

- This policy is reviewed at least once a year and after any significant change in regulation, technology or business.
- Non-compliance is reported to the Compliance Officer and handled under the disciplinary procedure.
